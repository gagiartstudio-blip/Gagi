Overview

Econiq is a conceptual sustainability brand built to make ethical commerce feel premium and modern. The project explores identity, messaging, and retail experiences without defaulting to common green tropes.

Positioning

A resilient, performance-led sustainability narrative that positions the brand as both aspirational and responsible.

Visual Language

Confident typography, premium color transitions, and distinctive iconography that scale across products and campaigns.

Experience

A cohesive brand system expressed across packaging, digital, retail, and social touchpoints with consistent clarity and premium appeal.

Challenge

The main challenge was balancing premium commerce appeal with authentic sustainability messaging. The objective was to craft a brand that felt credible, elevated, and contemporary without relying on recycled or literal environmental visuals.

The goal was to shift from “recycled” cues toward a resilient, performance-led sustainability narrative.
  • Position the brand as both aspirational and responsible.
  • Create a visual system that works across packaging, web, and social media.
  • Ensure the identity supports premium product storytelling.

Approach

We combined audience insight, category analysis, and visual exploration to develop a flexible identity system. The approach prioritised clarity, contrast, and an elevated tone that can scale across products and campaign touchpoints.

Strategic Framework

Messaging that earns the claim

Built a messaging framework that supports premium sustainability claims with proof points rooted in real impact metrics, differentiated tone, and targeted audience segments.

Visual Identity

A system, not a logo

Designed a distinctive color, typography, and iconography system that adapts seamlessly across packaging, web, social, retail, and campaign touchpoints without losing brand coherence.

Solution

The final identity combines confident typography, premium color transitions, and simple visual systems that carry across product, digital, and retail experiences. The outcome is a brand that feels modern, trustworthy, and market-ready.

Reflection

Where the system landed.

The Econiq system proves that sustainable commerce can command premium positioning through confident visual language and authentic storytelling—without relying on predictable green aesthetics or overwhelming consumers with complexity.

Established a premium sustainable brand that supports credibility and differentiation.

Extend the system into packaging, campaign direction, and retail environment design.
